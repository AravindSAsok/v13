A work-time tracker built with Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase Auth, and PostgreSQL. It records punch and break timestamps, derives daily and weekly totals, and provides history, reports, and CSV exports. Its break policy is explicit: breaks under 15 minutes remain paid; breaks of 15 minutes or longer are deducted in full.
