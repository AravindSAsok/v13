An AI-powered research assistant that analyzes PDFs, retrieves relevant passages, and generates source-grounded answers with page citations. Answer Audit, Research Agent, Research Audit, Human Review, and a Retrieval Evaluation Lab support evidence inspection, claim review, and citation-quality checks.
