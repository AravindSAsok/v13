A document-grounded AI assistant for PDF and text files. Users can select documents, ask questions, and receive answers grounded in retrieved passages. It combines source-aware extraction and chunking, BM25-style retrieval, Gemini answer generation, citation validation, and Supabase-backed accounts, documents, and conversations.
